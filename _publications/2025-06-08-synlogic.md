**Abstract**: Logical reasoning is a core capability of human intelligence and a key area for improving artificial intelligence systems. However, existing datasets for logical reasoning are small and often have limited coverage of different types of logical structures. In this work, we introduce SynLogic, a framework for synthesizing large-scale, verifiable logical reasoning data by combining automated theorem proving with large language models. Our approach generates reasoning chains in first-order logic with guarantees of correctness, allowing us to create datasets orders of magnitude larger than existing benchmarks. We further demonstrate that models trained on SynLogic data achieve significant improvements in logical reasoning capabilities on downstream tasks, including both symbolic reasoning and natural language reasoning. SynLogic opens new avenues for building generalizable, verifiable AI systems.

*First author work conducted at HKUST NLP Group.*
