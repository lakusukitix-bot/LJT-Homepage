**Abstract**: We present C-Eval, a comprehensive, multi-level, multi-discipline Chinese evaluation suite for foundation models consisting of 13,948 multiple-choice questions from 52 disciplines spanning natural sciences, social sciences, humanities, and engineering. The benchmark is designed to capture the full spectrum of capabilities of large language models (LLMs) in Chinese, including knowledge recall, reasoning, and problem-solving. We evaluate 20+ state-of-the-art LLMs on C-Eval, revealing that current Chinese LLMs still have significant room for improvement, especially in advanced domain-specific reasoning tasks. We further analyze the correlation between performance on C-Eval and other benchmarks, providing insights into what capabilities LLMs need to perform well on domain-specific Chinese tasks.
